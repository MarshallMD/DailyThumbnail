Finnhub is a consolidated feed and can lag the primary exchange by a few seconds; BTC open and previous close are the nearest CoinGecko prints to 9:30 AM and 4:00 PM Eastern, not exchange prints. Before the opening bell a stock's current price equals its previous close, so the change will read zero until the market is trading. US market holidays are not in the calendar here, so on a holiday the previous close will be one session further back than this app assumes.
